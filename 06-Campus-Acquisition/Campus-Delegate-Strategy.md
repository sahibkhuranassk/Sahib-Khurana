# Campus Delegate Strategy

## Overview

Developed and managed a Campus Delegate (CD) program to create a student-led acquisition network across colleges and universities.

The program enabled students to act as campus-level marketing representatives and promote study-abroad opportunities, scholarships, and HEST campaigns within their own student communities.

## Objectives

- Build a scalable campus acquisition channel
- Increase HEST registrations
- Create student-to-student marketing
- Expand reach across colleges
- Generate awareness through campus communities
- Support digital campaigns with offline student networks

## Campus Delegate Role

Campus Delegates were responsible for:

- Promoting HEST within their college
- Sharing campaign content on social media
- Promoting campaigns in WhatsApp groups
- Connecting with relevant student communities
- Reaching out to placement cells / TPOs
- Supporting campus-level awareness
- Creating student-generated content
- Sharing campaign registration links

## Recruitment Strategy

Campus Delegates were recruited through:

- Student communities
- College networks
- Social media
- Internship platforms
- Existing student networks
- Campus-level referrals

## Onboarding

New Campus Delegates were provided with:

- Campaign information
- Communication guidelines
- Promotional creatives
- Registration links
- Expected activities
- Performance expectations
- Campaign timelines

## Activity Framework

Campus Delegates could execute activities such as:

### Digital

- WhatsApp group promotion
- Instagram stories
- Instagram posts
- Reels
- Student community sharing
- Direct student outreach

### Campus

- College outreach
- Placement-cell/TPO communication
- Student community engagement
- Campus posters
- Peer-to-peer promotion
- VoxPop content

## Performance Management

Delegate performance was monitored using:

- Registrations generated
- Activity completion
- Communication frequency
- Student engagement
- Content output
- Campus outreach
- Lead contribution
