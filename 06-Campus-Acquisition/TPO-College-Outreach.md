# TPO & College Outreach

## Overview

Built college-level outreach processes to connect study-abroad campaigns with placement cells, Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), faculty, and student communities.

## Objective

- Build institutional relationships
- Reach larger student audiences
- Promote study-abroad opportunities
- Support HEST registrations
- Create additional acquisition channels
- Extend campaign reach beyond paid media

## Outreach Targets

The outreach process focused on:

- Colleges
- Universities
- TPOs
- Placement cells
- Faculty members
- Student coordinators
- Student communities

## Outreach Process

```text
College Identification
        ↓
Contact Discovery
        ↓
TPO / Placement Cell Outreach
        ↓
Campaign Introduction
        ↓
Campaign Assets
        ↓
College-Level Promotion
        ↓
Student Reach
        ↓
Registrations
