# Campus Delegate Results

## Program Impact

The Campus Delegate program was developed as a student-led acquisition channel to extend campaign reach directly into college communities.

## Key Performance Target

**Monthly Registration Target:** 1,500 registrations

The target was designed to make Campus Delegates a measurable acquisition channel rather than treating the program purely as an awareness initiative.
