# Funnel Optimisation

## Overview

Worked across the marketing funnel to connect acquisition, lead qualification, communication, and conversion activities rather than treating lead generation as an isolated function.

## Funnel Structure

The acquisition funnel was structured across multiple stages:

1. Awareness
2. Interest
3. Lead Generation
4. Lead Qualification
5. Follow-up
6. Conversion
7. Re-engagement

## Acquisition

Students were acquired through multiple channels including:

- Meta Ads
- Google Ads
- Influencer Marketing
- WhatsApp Marketing
- Campus Delegates
- Events
- Social Media
- Existing Lead Database
- Scholarship-focused campaigns

## Lead Qualification

Lead quality was improved by identifying relevant student attributes such as:

- Study-abroad intent
- Preferred destination
- Course interest
- Intake timeline
- Funding requirement
- Scholarship interest
- Education-loan requirement

## Follow-Up

Lead follow-up was supported through:

- WhatsApp communication
- Email campaigns
- Calling
- CRM workflows
- Campaign-specific communication
- Re-engagement campaigns

## Re-Engagement

Existing student databases were used to create additional opportunities through targeted campaigns.

Re-engagement initiatives included:

- Scholarship campaigns
- HEST promotions
- SAFE event communication
- Education-loan campaigns
- Student benefit campaigns
- Referral campaigns

## Funnel Optimisation Approach

The funnel was continuously reviewed to identify potential drop-offs between:

**Acquisition → Lead → Qualification → Follow-up → Conversion**

Optimisation focused on:

- Improving messaging relevance
- Matching campaigns to student intent
- Strengthening lead qualification
- Improving follow-up communication
- Re-engaging older leads
- Connecting marketing activity with downstream conversion

## Cross-Channel Approach

Different channels were used for different stages of the funnel rather than relying on a single acquisition source.

For example:

- Paid media → Acquisition
- Influencers → Awareness & Acquisition
- Campus Delegates → Campus Acquisition
- WhatsApp → Acquisition & Follow-up
- CRM → Qualification & Re-engagement
- Events → Awareness, Engagement & Lead Generation

## Key Learning

A scalable growth system requires alignment between acquisition channels, lead quality, follow-up processes, CRM operations, and conversion teams. Increasing lead volume alone does not necessarily improve business outcomes.
