# Creative Testing & Optimisation

## Overview

Built a structured approach to testing marketing creatives across paid acquisition campaigns, with the objective of identifying messaging and creative angles that could improve lead generation and campaign efficiency.

## Testing Objectives

- Identify high-performing creative concepts
- Improve lead-generation performance
- Test different student motivations
- Reduce dependency on a single creative
- Develop repeatable creative testing frameworks

## Creative Angles Tested

### Financial Benefit

Focused on scholarships, education-loan benefits, savings, discounts, and financial support.

### Opportunity

Highlighted access to scholarships, study-abroad opportunities, and student benefits.

### Problem-Solution

Addressed common student concerns around:

- Education costs
- Funding
- Scholarships
- Loan availability
- Study-abroad planning

### Urgency

Used deadlines, registration windows, and limited opportunities where applicable.

### Social Proof

Used student-oriented messaging and campaign credibility to improve trust.

## Creative Formats

Testing included:

- Static images
- Short-form videos
- Reels
- Educational creatives
- Benefit-led creatives
- Direct-response creatives
- Information-led creatives

## Testing Process

1. Identify campaign objective
2. Define the audience problem
3. Develop multiple creative angles
4. Create variations of the messaging
5. Launch creatives within relevant campaigns
6. Monitor performance
7. Compare creative-level results
8. Identify patterns in high-performing concepts
9. Iterate messaging and visual direction
10. Scale successful approaches

## Performance Indicators

Creative performance was evaluated using:

- Leads
- Cost per lead
- Registration volume
- Conversion rate
- Engagement
- Click-through behaviour
- Creative-level efficiency

## Optimisation Approach

Instead of evaluating creatives only on engagement, performance was considered in relation to the campaign objective and downstream lead quality.

Optimisation decisions included:

- Refreshing weak creatives
- Developing variations of stronger concepts
- Testing new messaging angles
- Refining calls to action
- Aligning creative messaging with landing pages
- Removing inefficient creative variations

## Key Learning

Creative testing is not only about finding the most engaging advertisement. The objective is to identify creative concepts that attract the right audience and contribute to meaningful conversions.
