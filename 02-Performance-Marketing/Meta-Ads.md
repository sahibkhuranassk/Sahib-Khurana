# Meta Ads & Performance Marketing

## Overview

Managed and supported Meta advertising initiatives focused on student acquisition, lead generation, campaign growth, and conversion-oriented marketing.

## Objectives

- Generate qualified study-abroad leads
- Drive HEST registrations
- Promote education-finance products and services
- Support event registrations
- Improve campaign efficiency
- Build scalable paid acquisition systems

## Campaigns

Key campaign categories included:

- HEST Scholarship Campaigns
- SAFE Event Campaigns
- Education Loan Campaigns
- Scholarship Campaigns
- Study-Abroad Lead Generation
- Student Benefit & Offer Campaigns

## Campaign Strategy

Campaign planning involved:

1. Defining the target audience
2. Developing campaign objectives
3. Creating audience segments
4. Developing creative concepts
5. Writing ad copy and messaging
6. Launching campaigns
7. Monitoring performance
8. Identifying high-performing creatives
9. Optimising campaigns based on performance
10. Scaling successful approaches

## Creative Strategy

Developed and tested creative concepts around:

- Scholarship opportunities
- Study-abroad aspirations
- Education loan benefits
- Loan comparison
- Student savings
- Event registrations
- Urgency and deadline-based messaging

Creative formats included:

- Static creatives
- Short-form video
- Reels
- Educational content
- Benefit-led creatives
- Problem-solution creatives
- Direct-response creatives

## Audience Strategy

Audience planning considered:

- Study-abroad aspirants
- Students researching international education
- Students interested in scholarships
- Students exploring education financing
- Students planning upcoming intakes
- Retargeting opportunities from existing marketing audiences

## Optimisation Framework

Campaign performance was reviewed across:

- Leads
- Cost per lead
- Lead quality
- Registration volume
- Conversion rate
- Creative performance
- Audience performance
- Campaign-level efficiency

Optimisation actions included:

- Creative iteration
- Audience refinement
- Budget adjustments
- Testing different messaging angles
- Identifying high-performing campaigns
- Reducing inefficient campaign elements

## Collaboration

Worked with creative, performance marketing, content, and growth teams to align:

- Campaign objectives
- Ad creatives
- Landing pages
- Lead-generation flows
- CRM follow-up
- Conversion objectives

## Key Learning

Performance marketing works best when paid acquisition is connected to the complete funnel — from creative and targeting to lead qualification, CRM follow-up, and final conversion.
