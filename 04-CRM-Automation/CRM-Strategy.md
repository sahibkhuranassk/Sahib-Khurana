# CRM & Marketing Automation Strategy

## Overview

Built and improved CRM processes to connect marketing acquisition with lead qualification, nurturing, follow-up, reactivation, and conversion.

## Objective

- Improve lead management
- Create structured follow-up processes
- Reduce lead leakage
- Segment leads based on intent
- Improve communication consistency
- Support marketing-to-sales conversion
- Reactivate existing databases
