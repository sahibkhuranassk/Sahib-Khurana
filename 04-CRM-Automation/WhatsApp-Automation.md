# WhatsApp Marketing & Automation

## Overview

Developed structured WhatsApp communication workflows for student acquisition, lead nurturing, campaign promotion, and re-engagement.

## Objectives

- Reach students through relevant communities
- Distribute campaign communication efficiently
- Nurture existing leads
- Drive registrations
- Re-engage inactive leads
- Support campaign-specific follow-ups

## Communication Types

### Campaign Promotion

Used WhatsApp to communicate:

- HEST
- SAFE
- Scholarships
- Education loans
- Student benefits
- Referral opportunities

### Lead Nurturing

Communication was structured around the student's position in the funnel.

Example:

```text
New Lead
   ↓
Initial Communication
   ↓
Value Proposition
   ↓
Follow-up
   ↓
Qualification
   ↓
Counselling / Sales
   ↓
