# Email Marketing & Automation

## Overview

Used email as a structured communication channel for campaign promotion, lead nurturing, reminders, re-engagement, and lifecycle communication.

## Objectives

- Nurture student leads
- Support campaign registrations
- Communicate relevant opportunities
- Improve follow-up consistency
- Reactivate existing leads
- Support conversion journeys
