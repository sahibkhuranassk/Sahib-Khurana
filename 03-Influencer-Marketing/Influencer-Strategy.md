# Influencer Marketing Strategy

## Overview

Developed an influencer-led acquisition strategy focused on generating study-abroad student leads through nano, micro, student, and campus influencers.

## Campaign Objective

- Generate qualified student leads
- Expand organic reach among study-abroad audiences
- Build a scalable influencer acquisition channel
- Reduce dependency on a single paid acquisition source
- Establish repeatable influencer partnerships

## Campaign Budget

**Planned Budget:** ₹25,00,000

**Campaign Period:** November 2025 – August 2026

**Lead Target:** 10,000–12,000 leads

The budget was planned around performance-oriented influencer partnerships, with greater emphasis on measurable lead generation rather than reach alone.

## Influencer Strategy

The strategy focused primarily on:

- Nano influencers
- Micro influencers
- Student influencers
- Campus influencers
- Study-abroad creators
- Education-focused creators

The objective was to access highly relevant student communities through creators who already had trust and familiarity within their audience.

## Influencer Selection

Influencers were evaluated based on:

- Audience relevance
- Student audience composition
- Study-abroad relevance
- Engagement
- Content quality
- Expected lead potential
- Commercial viability
- Previous campaign performance

## Commercial Model

Influencer partnerships were structured using performance-oriented commercial models wherever practical.

Different influencer tiers were assigned different payout structures based on expected reach, audience quality, and lead-generation potential.

## Campaign Execution

The execution process included:

1. Identifying relevant influencers
2. Shortlisting creators
3. Evaluating audience quality
4. Negotiating commercials
5. Sharing campaign briefs
6. Providing messaging guidelines
7. Tracking influencer activity
8. Monitoring lead generation
9. Comparing creator performance
10. Optimising future allocations

## Tracking

Influencer performance was monitored using:

- Unique tracking links
- Lead attribution
- Creator-wise lead counts
- Cost per lead
- Lead quality
- Conversion performance

## Content Approach

Influencers were encouraged to communicate campaigns in a creator-native format rather than relying only on standard promotional messaging.

Content themes included:

- Study-abroad opportunities
- Scholarships
- Education loans
- Student funding
- Application experiences
- Financial planning
- HEST
- SAFE

## Optimisation

Influencer performance was reviewed at the creator level.

The optimisation process focused on:

- Identifying high-performing creators
- Comparing cost versus lead output
- Improving campaign briefs
- Testing different content formats
- Refining creator selection
- Reallocating budget toward stronger-performing partnerships

## Key Learning

Influencer marketing becomes more scalable when creators are treated as measurable acquisition channels rather than only awareness partners. Audience relevance, attribution, lead quality, and cost efficiency are important inputs when evaluating partnerships.
