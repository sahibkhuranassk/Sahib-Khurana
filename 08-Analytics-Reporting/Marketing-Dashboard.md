# Marketing Dashboard & Reporting

## Overview

Used structured marketing reporting to track acquisition performance, campaign progress, lead movement, and conversion outcomes across multiple marketing channels.

## Reporting Objectives

- Track campaign performance against targets
- Identify high-performing acquisition sources
- Monitor registration and lead volume
- Track sanctions and downstream outcomes
- Compare source-level performance
- Identify gaps and opportunities
- Support data-driven campaign decisions

## Core KPIs

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Registrations | Measure campaign acquisition |
| Leads | Measure lead generation |
| Qualified Leads | Measure lead quality |
| Sanctions | Measure downstream conversion |
| Conversion Rate | Measure funnel efficiency |
| Cost per Lead | Measure acquisition efficiency |
| Budget | Track campaign investment |
| Target Achievement | Compare actual vs target |

## Source-Level Reporting

Campaign performance was reviewed across acquisition sources such as:

- Meta Ads
- Influencer Marketing
- Campus Delegates
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Organic / Social
- Other Sources
