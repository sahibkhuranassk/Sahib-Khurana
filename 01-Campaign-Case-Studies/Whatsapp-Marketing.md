# WhatsApp Marketing Case Study

## Campaign Overview

Built and managed a structured WhatsApp marketing system to reach study-abroad students through relevant communities, generate conversations, and support lead acquisition.

## Objective

- Generate qualified student leads
- Increase reach through WhatsApp communities
- Build a repeatable organic acquisition channel
- Drive students toward study-abroad and education-finance conversations
- Maintain multiple WhatsApp accounts while reducing account restrictions

## Strategy

The strategy focused on community-based distribution rather than relying only on paid acquisition.

Key components included:

- WhatsApp group discovery
- Relevant student community participation
- Structured posting schedules
- Lead identification and qualification
- Direct student conversations
- Campaign-based messaging
- Account-level activity management
- Regular group sanitisation

## Execution

Managed multiple WhatsApp numbers and organised relevant student groups across accounts.

The operating process included:

1. Identifying relevant student communities
2. Joining suitable groups
3. Maintaining group lists
4. Publishing campaign messages
5. Monitoring responses
6. Identifying interested students
7. Moving relevant conversations toward lead qualification
8. Tracking activity and performance

## Campaign Channels

- WhatsApp Groups
- WhatsApp Status
- Direct Messages
- Existing Student Lead Database
- Campaign-specific Messaging

## Messaging Approach

Different messages were developed depending on the campaign objective, including:

- Scholarships
- HEST registrations
- Education loans
- SAFE registrations
- Study-abroad funding
- Student offers and benefits

## Challenges

- WhatsApp account restrictions
- Group limits
- Spam-sensitive environments
- Maintaining consistent activity across multiple accounts
- Identifying relevant groups
- Keeping communities clean and relevant

## Optimisation

To improve operational efficiency:

- Group lists were organised systematically
- Posting activity was distributed across accounts
- Irrelevant and spam-heavy groups were removed
- Messaging was structured around campaign objectives
- Existing leads were re-engaged through WhatsApp

## Key Learning

WhatsApp can function as a scalable acquisition and re-engagement channel when community discovery, messaging, lead qualification, and account management are treated as a structured growth system.
