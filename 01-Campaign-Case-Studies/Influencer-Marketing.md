# Influencer Marketing Case Study

## Campaign Overview

The influencer marketing program was designed to use creators and student-focused communities to generate awareness and qualified leads for study-abroad and education-finance campaigns.

The strategy focused on moving beyond traditional celebrity influencers and building a scalable network of nano, micro, student, and campus influencers.

## Campaign Objective

- Generate qualified student leads through influencers
- Reach study-abroad audiences through trusted creators
- Build a scalable influencer acquisition channel
- Reduce dependence on a single marketing channel
- Test different creator profiles and audience segments
- Track influencer performance based on lead generation

## Target Audience

The primary audience included:

- Students planning to study abroad
- Students researching universities and courses
- Students looking for scholarships
- Students exploring education-loan options
- Students preparing for upcoming intakes
- Young audiences interested in international education

## Campaign Budget

Planned campaign budget:

**₹25,00,000**

Campaign period:

**November 2025 – August 2026**

Lead-generation target:

**10,000–11,000 leads**

The budget was structured to test multiple influencer categories and identify scalable acquisition opportunities.

## Influencer Strategy

Instead of relying primarily on large creators, the strategy focused on building a distributed creator network.

### Influencer Categories

- Nano influencers
- Micro influencers
- Student influencers
- Campus influencers
- Education creators
- Study-abroad creators
- Relevant niche creators

## Creator Selection

Influencers were evaluated based on:

- Audience relevance
- Student audience composition
- Engagement
- Content quality
- Geographic relevance
- Study-abroad relevance
- Expected lead potential
- Commercial viability

The objective was to prioritise audience relevance over follower count alone.

## Campaign Execution

### 1. Influencer Identification

Potential creators were identified across relevant student, education, study-abroad, and lifestyle audiences.

### 2. Outreach

Creators were contacted and introduced to the campaign proposition.

The outreach process included:

- Creator discovery
- Initial outreach
- Campaign explanation
- Commercial discussions
- Deliverable discussions
- Confirmation and onboarding

### 3. Campaign Briefing

Selected influencers were provided with campaign information and communication guidelines.

The brief covered:

- Campaign objective
- Key proposition
- Target audience
- Required messaging
- Content format
- Call-to-action
- Lead-generation mechanism

### 4. Content Execution

Influencers created content in formats such as:

- Instagram Reels
- Stories
- Educational videos
- Student-focused posts
- Campaign announcements
- Direct-response content

### 5. Lead Tracking

Influencer-driven leads were tracked to understand the contribution of individual creators.

Key tracking parameters included:

- Influencer name
- Content/campaign
- Leads generated
- Lead quality
- Cost
- Conversion potential
- Overall performance

## Performance-Based Approach

The program explored performance-linked influencer payouts rather than relying entirely on fixed commercial fees.

This allowed creator economics to be connected more closely with measurable acquisition outcomes.

Different influencer tiers were evaluated based on expected reach, audience quality, and lead-generation potential.
