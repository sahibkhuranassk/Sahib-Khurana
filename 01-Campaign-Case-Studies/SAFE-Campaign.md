# SAFE Campaign Case Study

## Campaign Overview

SAFE (Study Abroad Funding Expo) is an offline student acquisition and education-finance campaign designed to bring study-abroad students into a high-intent environment where they can explore scholarships, education loans, and funding options.

The campaign combined event marketing, digital acquisition, social media, WhatsApp, influencer outreach, campus outreach, and lead-nurturing activities.

## Campaign Objective

- Generate registrations for SAFE
- Reach students actively planning to study abroad
- Create high-intent education-finance leads
- Drive offline event attendance
- Increase awareness of scholarships and education-loan options
- Create opportunities for post-event counselling and conversions

## Target Audience

Primary audience:

- Students planning to study abroad
- Students researching education loans
- Students looking for scholarships and funding options
- Students preparing for upcoming international university intakes
- Parents and students evaluating education-finance options

## Campaign Proposition

The campaign positioned SAFE as a single destination where students could explore:

- 26,000+ scholarships
- 1,000+ fully funded scholarships
- Education-loan options from multiple banks
- On-spot loan assistance
- Scholarship guidance
- Study-abroad funding support
- Seminars and student-focused sessions

## Campaign Strategy

The campaign used a multi-channel acquisition strategy.

### 1. Performance Marketing

Digital campaigns were used to reach students based on study-abroad intent, education, country, course, and funding-related interests.

Key activities:

- Meta advertising
- Campaign-specific creatives
- Lead-generation campaigns
- Retargeting and audience nurturing
- Creative testing and optimisation

### 2. Social Media

Organic social media was used to build awareness and create urgency around the event.

Content included:

- Event announcements
- Registration posts
- Reels
- Scholarship-led content
- Education-loan content
- Event highlights
- Countdown communication
- Student-focused educational content

### 3. Influencer Marketing

Student and education-focused influencers were used to extend campaign reach and introduce SAFE to relevant audiences.

Activities included:

- Influencer identification
- Briefing and content coordination
- Campaign communication
- Tracking influencer-driven registrations
- Performance evaluation

### 4. WhatsApp Marketing

WhatsApp was used as a direct-response channel for reaching existing student audiences.

Activities included:

- Registration messages
- Event reminders
- Follow-up communication
- Student queries
- Group-based distribution
- Lead nurturing

### 5. Campus & College Outreach

The campaign also used campus-level acquisition channels.

Activities included:

- Campus delegate outreach
- College communication
- Placement-cell/TPO outreach
- Student community promotion
- Campus-level event awareness

## Event Locations

SAFE was planned as a multi-city campaign covering major student markets.

Locations included:

- Bangalore
- Hyderabad
- Mumbai
- Chennai
- Delhi
- Kozhikode
- Kochi

## Execution Framework

The campaign was managed through multiple stages:

### Awareness

Build awareness around SAFE and its value proposition.

### Acquisition

Drive students to registration through paid, organic, influencer, WhatsApp, and campus channels.

### Registration

Capture student information and maintain the registration database.

### Reminder & Nurturing

Use WhatsApp, email, calls, and other communication channels to improve event attendance.

### Event Conversion

Connect students with scholarship and education-finance opportunities during the event.

### Post-Event Follow-up

Continue communication with relevant students after the event for counselling and education-finance requirements.

## Key Learnings

- Multi-channel acquisition creates multiple entry points into the student funnel.
- Event campaigns require strong pre-event nurturing, not just registration generation.
- Channel-level tracking helps identify scalable acquisition sources.
- Creative messaging needs to communicate both the event value and the student's immediate benefit.
- WhatsApp and campus networks can complement paid acquisition by reaching high-intent student communities.
- Post-registration communication is critical for improving the transition from registration to attendance.

## Campaign Takeaway

SAFE demonstrated an integrated approach to student acquisition by combining digital marketing, influencer marketing, campus outreach, WhatsApp communication, and offline events into a single acquisition funnel.

The campaign strengthened the connection between study-abroad awareness, scholarship discovery, and education-finance assistance.
