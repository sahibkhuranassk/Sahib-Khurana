# Social Media & Content Strategy

## Overview

Developed and managed content strategies designed to build brand awareness, educate student audiences, support campaign objectives, and generate engagement across social platforms.

## Objectives

- Build brand awareness
- Educate study-abroad audiences
- Support campaign acquisition
- Increase audience engagement
- Create consistent content output
- Connect organic content with broader marketing campaigns

## Content Pillars

### Scholarships

Content focused on:

- Scholarship opportunities
- Fully funded scholarships
- Eligibility
- Deadlines
- Application guidance
- Scholarship awareness

### Study Abroad

Content around:

- Countries
- Universities
- Courses
- Student experiences
- Application journeys
- Study-abroad planning

### Education Finance

Content explaining:

- Education loans
- Interest rates
- Loan benefits
- Funding options
- Financial planning
- Student finance

### Campaign Content

Campaign-specific content supporting:

- HEST
- SAFE
- Scholarship campaigns
- Education-loan campaigns
- Student offers

### Educational Content

Content designed to answer common student questions and simplify complex study-abroad and funding topics.

## Content Formats

- Instagram Reels
- Short-form videos
- Static posts
- Carousels
- Stories
- Educational posts
- Campaign creatives
- Student-focused content

## Content Planning

Content was organised through structured calendars covering:

- Campaign priorities
- Educational topics
- Trending opportunities
- Promotional content
- Engagement content
- Student questions
- Important deadlines
