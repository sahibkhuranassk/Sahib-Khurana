# Content Calendar & Planning

## Overview

Created structured content calendars to maintain consistent social media communication while aligning content with campaigns, audience interests, and business priorities.

## Planning Objectives

- Maintain consistent publishing
- Align content with marketing campaigns
- Balance educational and promotional content
- Identify relevant content opportunities
- Support campaign launches
- Improve content organisation

## Content Mix

The calendar can include:

- Educational content
- Scholarship content
- Study-abroad content
- Education-finance content
- Campaign promotions
- Student stories
- Engagement content
- Trending topics
- Reels
- Carousels
- Static posts
- Stories

## Weekly Structure

A typical weekly content mix can include:

| Day | Content Focus |
|---|---|
| Monday | Scholarship / Educational |
| Tuesday | Study Abroad |
| Wednesday | Education Finance |
| Thursday | Engagement / Trending |
| Friday | Campaign / Promotional |
| Saturday | Student / Lifestyle |
| Sunday | Community / Recap |

## Content Planning Process

1. Identify monthly business priorities
2. Map active campaigns
3. Identify audience questions
4. Select content pillars
5. Develop content ideas
6. Assign formats
7. Write copy and hooks
8. Coordinate creatives
9. Schedule publishing
10. Monitor performance
11. Optimise future content

Content calendars were aligned with major campaigns such as:

Performance can be reviewed using:

- Leads

## Optimisation

Content planning can be improved by identifying:

- High-performing topics
- Strong hooks
- Successful formats
- Audience questions
- High-engagement themes
- Content contributing to campaign objectives

## Key Learning

A strong content calendar should balance consistency with flexibility. Content planning works best when the calendar is connected to business priorities while leaving room for timely opportunities and audience feedback.
