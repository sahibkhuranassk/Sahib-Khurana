# Social Media Campaigns

## Overview

Planned and executed social media campaigns designed to support brand awareness, student acquisition, campaign registrations, and audience engagement.

## Campaign Categories

### Scholarship Campaigns

Content focused on scholarship discovery, eligibility, deadlines, and opportunities for students planning to study abroad.

### HEST Campaigns

Social content supporting HEST registration and awareness.

Content themes included:

- Scholarship opportunity
- ₹2 Crore scholarship fund
- Up to ₹3 Lakhs per student
- Free registration
- Online aptitude test
- Registration deadlines

### SAFE Campaigns

Social media communication supporting SAFE event registrations and awareness.

Content included:

- Event announcements
- City-specific communication
- Scholarship messaging
- Education-loan benefits
- Event countdowns
- Registration reminders

### Education Finance

Educational and promotional content covering:

- Education loans
- Loan benefits
- Interest rates
- Funding options
- Financial planning
- Study-abroad financing

## Content Formats

- Reels
- Carousels
- Static posts
- Stories
- Educational videos
- Campaign announcements
- Promotional creatives
- Short-form videos
